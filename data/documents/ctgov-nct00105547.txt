Educational GapForge corpus stub for NCT00105547 (tarenflurbil / Flurizan). This Phase 3 program evaluated cognitive and functional endpoints such as ADAS-Cog and ADCS-ADL over approximately 18 months in mild Alzheimer disease. Public reports indicate primary efficacy endpoints were not met and development of Flurizan for AD was discontinued.
